Waitlist privacy notice

Who we are: Obsanity Labs LLC, operating as Obsanity Labs ("we"). Contact: privacy@obsanity.org.

What we collect: your email address, the product you chose, the time you consented, and technical fields needed to run the form (campaign codes, an optional referral code you typed, and a short-lived network-address hash used only to block abuse). We do not ask for your name, wallet, phone number, or payment details.

Why: to send you a confirmation and, if you confirm, product updates and an early-access notice for that one product. The legal basis is your consent.

How long: unconfirmed addresses are deleted after 7 days. Confirmed addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or for 24 months after our last email to you, whichever comes first. We then keep only a hashed suppression record for 3 years so we do not email you again.

Who else: email is sent through Google LLC (Google Workspace), which processes it on our instructions. Google LLC stores message data in the US. We do not sell your email. We do not use advertising pixels on this page.

Your choices: every email has an unsubscribe link. You may also email privacy@obsanity.org to access, correct, or delete your address. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may complain to your data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of email already sent.

Children: this waitlist is not for anyone under 16.

This notice covers the waitlist only. It does not cover a product account, a wallet connection, or any future paid feature.
