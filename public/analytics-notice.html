What we collect

If you opt in, we store on your device a random identifier (anonymous_id) and a session identifier, and we send: pages you view (path only; query strings are removed), the referring site's host (not the full URL), first-touch campaign parameters you arrived with, and your consent state. We also record product-use events from a fixed list (for example feature use, experiment runs, and share actions). On phreaking.fun only, we may record whether a wallet is connected (yes or no), the wallet type you chose, and the integer chain id. We do not collect wallet addresses, transaction hashes, ENS names, email addresses, names, or free-text messages. A truncated IP address is used for a few seconds to filter bots and is not stored. This information is pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data.

Why

Product analytics and aggregated traction reporting only. We do not use it for advertising, sale, cross-context sharing, or enrichment.

Legal basis

For visitors in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), and the ePrivacy rules on storage on your device). Nothing is stored or sent for analytics before you opt in. You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it, from the consent settings on this page below. Withdrawal stops new collection immediately. For other visitors: we collect this only as described in this notice, and you can opt out by the same control. If we cannot tell where you are, we ask for consent first.

How long

Event-level records are kept for at most 90 days, then deleted. We do not keep a backup copy of those raw records. Aggregated counts that can no longer be linked to your identifier may be kept longer.
